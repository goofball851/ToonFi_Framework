# 🌐 ToonFi — Genre Framework  
*A framework for interactive cartoon worlds*

---

## 1. What is ToonFi?

**ToonFi is a genre of interactive cartoons where NFT collections become explorable worlds.**

In ToonFi, a cartoon is not only a show.  
It is a **place.**

Websites are not menus.  
They are **locations inside the fiction.**

Episodes are not the starting point.  
They are **discoveries within a world.**

ToonFi blends:

- cartoon storytelling  
- explorable environments  
- living characters  
- collectible objects  
- and interactive systems  

into continuous cartoon spaces that exist before, during, and after any episode.

---

## 2. Core Genre Pillars

### 🗺️ World-First  
ToonFi always begins with a **world**, not content.

If you remove all videos, the world should still exist.

---

### 🎞️ Cartoons as Discoveries  
Episodes are transmissions, memories, broadcasts, or artifacts found inside the world.

They can stand alone.  
Inside ToonFi, they gain context.

---

### 🧬 Living Cartoon Systems  
ToonFi favors idle life, background behavior, roaming characters, evolving objects, and persistent states.

Nothing important should feel like a static page.

---

### 🧩 Collectibles as World Objects  
NFTs are companions, keys, devices, mutations, and memories.

Ownership enables **continuity**, not paywalls.

---

### 📖 Narrative as Geography  
Lore is walked through, uncovered, archived, overheard, and collected.

Story is layered into spaces and systems.

---

### 🔁 Expansion-Native  
Every ToonFi world is designed to expand into cartoons, games, memes, toys, tools, and experiments.

ToonFi worlds are narrative engines.

---

## 3. ToonFi Layer Model

**SOCIAL LAYER**
Clips, loops, memes, live scenes

**INTERFACE LAYER**
Sites, maps, rooms, viewers

**PHENOMENA LAYER**
Events, experiments, world systems

**NARRATIVE LAYER**
Lore, logs, memory, canon

**OBJECT LAYER**
Characters, creatures, devices, zones

**IDENTITY LAYER**
Profiles, collectibles, continuity

---

## 4. ToonFi Objects

A ToonFi object is not art.  
It is a **living entity.**

It may include:

- visual form  
- idle behavior  
- multiple states  
- interaction  
- memory  
- system relationships  
- change over time  

Examples: characters, devices, organisms, signals, locations, artifacts.

---

## 5. ToonFi Phenomena

A phenomenon is **something that happens in the world.**

It:

- exists over time  
- passes through phases  
- hosts objects  
- produces outcomes  
- leaves residue  

Phenomena make worlds feel alive.

---

## 6. ToonFi Cartoons

A ToonFi-compatible cartoon is a **window into a ToonFi world.**

Episodes may:

- unlock locations  
- activate systems  
- introduce roaming entities  
- canonize events  
- seed collectibles  

---

## 7. What Makes Something ToonFi-Compatible

A project is ToonFi-compatible if it:

- treats its site as a world  
- treats characters as inhabitants  
- treats collectibles as objects  
- treats lore as spatial  
- treats episodes as discoveries  
- allows continuity to form  
- supports expansion across formats

---

## 8. Design Philosophy

- World before content  
- Place before playlist  
- Living before static  
- Systems before pages  
- Exploration before feeds  
- Ownership as memory  
- Cartoons as environments  

---

## 9. Core Promise

ToonFi does not ask:

“What happens in this episode?”

It asks:

“What kind of place is this cartoon?”

---

## 10. One-Sentence Definition

**ToonFi is a genre of interactive cartoons where NFT collections become explorable worlds and stories are discovered by inhabiting them.**
